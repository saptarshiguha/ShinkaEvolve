## Summary

This list contains 32 clothing brands recommended by HackerNews users in a discussion about durable, quality clothing. The recommendations span various price points and specialties, from budget-friendly options like Champion and Muji to premium brands like Patagonia and Nudie Jeans. Common themes include:

- **Durability and longevity** (many brands mentioned for lasting 5+ years)
- **Natural materials** (emphasis on merino wool, organic cotton, and undyed fabrics)
- **Made in USA/Portugal** (several brands highlighted for domestic or European manufacturing)
- **Lifetime warranties** (Darn Tough, Nudie Jeans offer repair programs)
- **Sustainable practices** (Patagonia, Industry of All Nations, Unbleached Apparel)

Note: Several brands like Standard Issue, Carhartt, and Uniqlo received mixed feedback about declining quality in recent years, though they remain recommended with caveats.
